# Out of Scope

- Collaboration
- Cloud sync
- Mobile apps
- Agents and tool calling
- Voice, OCR
- Embeddings and RAG
- Plugin marketplace
- Team management

Out-of-scope items are intentionally deferred to avoid architectural overreach during MVP stabilization.

## Deferral Criteria

- Requires cross-device synchronization or account systems.
- Requires complex retrieval/index pipelines not required by MVP ACs.
- Requires broad platform expansion beyond macOS.

## Re-Entry Conditions

- MVP ACs are consistently met.
- Runtime and persistence behavior are stable under load.
- Risks in [[Risk_Register]] are reduced to acceptable levels.

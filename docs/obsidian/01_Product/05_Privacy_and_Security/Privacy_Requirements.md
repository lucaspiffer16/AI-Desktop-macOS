# Privacy Requirements

Privacy support is mandatory from MVP and should reduce accidental exposure during work sessions.

## Requirement Scope

- Provide fast-access privacy controls during active usage.
- Preserve user context when entering and exiting privacy state.
- Keep limitations explicit in product messaging.

## Validation Focus

- Verify privacy toggle latency and fallback behavior.
- Verify restored state consistency after privacy exit.
